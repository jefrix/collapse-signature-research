1. **Credit is now pricing what the ratings won't.** Oracle's 5-year CDS reached about 300 bp, near single-B levels, while all three agencies hold it at investment grade. Credit desks have priced a hyperscaler CDS basket as a separate risk category from banks.
2. **The financing stack has become visible.** Named SPVs, tenants and bank syndicates now appear in public, along with lender covenants written around principal behaviour: Crux AI, CSDC Finance/Anviran, and the SpaceX "Project Saturn" chip SPV. The "footnote opacity" of Cycle III has mostly ended.

Dalio's stated mechanism for puncturing a bubble (rising rates plus debt-financed capex) now matches the observable conditions. This is the first time in the project that a principal's stated mechanism and the ledger's data point the same way at the same time.

This synthesis is still hypothesis work. None of the above is a collapse prediction for any named institution.

## Part I — Proposed Rulings

### Ruling 6 (proposed): Rating–Market Divergence as a named signature element

The Oracle case now shows a sustained gap between the agency rating (BBB-/Baa2) and the market-implied rating (about single-B). S&P's reported reasoning cites management's "commitment to investment grade." That reasoning rests on a stated intent rather than on the balance sheet.

- **Historical analogue to test:** late-Qing foreign-loan pricing against official creditworthiness, and Ottoman Public Debt Administration spreads before 1875. In both cases, formal standing outlived market belief. *DeepSeek to check whether these analogues hold before this is adopted.*
- **Falsifiable form:** if the divergence is a signature element, it resolves by **rating convergence downward** (a cut to junk) rather than by spreads tightening back to IG levels. If spreads return below about 150 bp with no rating action, the element is falsified for this case.
- **Status:** PROPOSED. Adopt only after the analogue check.

### Ruling 7 (proposed): Behavioural Covenants as a Vehicle-Disclosure sub-type

The lender covenant on SpaceX's Project Saturn reportedly restricts *where the collateral chips may physically be* (not in orbit). It is the first observed case of creditors pricing the behaviour of one named principal into the debt documents of an AI-infrastructure vehicle. Add it as a sub-type in the Vehicle Disclosure watch.

- **Caveat:** it is unclear whether the covenant made it into the final documents. Confidence C until the documents or major-outlet confirmation appear.

### Ruling 8 (proposed): Gate-as-Function vs. Gate-as-Failure, a coding rule for liquidity gates

BCRED's gate is the trigger the Schwarzman dossier specified. But a gate working as designed is not a run. Proposed coding rule:

- A gate counts as a **signature event** only if (a) it persists for 3 or more consecutive quarters, **or** (b) the backlog of unfulfilled requests grows quarter on quarter, **or** (c) NAV marks are revised down in step with the gating.
- On current data, Q2 and Q3 meet none of (a) to (c): the backlog is roughly flat, about 75% of requested capital was returned within about 90 days, and liquidity exceeds $17B.
- **Result:** the signal is TRIGGERED as written, but its signature weight is LOW until Q4 2026 data arrives (expected early Dec).

### Carry-forward: NVIDIA supplier-or-node is unresolved in both directions

Since the Sep 18 ledger, two pieces of evidence point opposite ways:
- **Towards node:** supply commitments rose from $279B to $390B in one quarter.
- **Away from node:** the interim CEO (Kress) has said NVIDIA will take part in financing "selectively," with support concentrated on land, power and shell.

The principal departure (Huang's resignation after a code-of-conduct finding) is **not** a collapse-signature event. It is a governance shock unrelated to the capital-web thesis, and it should not be coded as self-indictment.

**Ruling:** leave the NVIDIA position open. Re-test at the Q4 FY27 report (Feb 2027) on whether credit-wrap and residual-value support continue under new leadership.
